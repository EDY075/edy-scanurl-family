# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with empathy and technical clarity;
- accept constructive feedback and focus on the user’s safety;
- respect privacy, consent and legal boundaries;
- avoid discriminatory, intimidating or disruptive conduct;
- do not publish personal data, credentials or exploit details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and interactions that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the private contact channel listed by the repository. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
